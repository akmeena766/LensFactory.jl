LensFactory.Lenses.get_critical_density — Function
get_critical_density(Dd::RV, Dds::RV, Ds::RV; unit="kg_m2")::RV

Calculate the critical surface density $(\Sigma_{\rm cr})$ given the angular diameter distances. The result can be returned in different units: "kg_m2" ${\rm (i. \: e., \: kg/m^2)}$, "msun_pc2" ${\rm (i. \: e., \: M_{\odot}/pc^2)}$, or "msun_arcsec2" ${\rm (i. \: e., \: M_{\odot}/arcsec^2)}$.
